# Organic Biophilic Warmth

**Design Name**: Organic Biophilic Warmth

**Concept**: Counter to cold finance. Earth tones, flowing organic shapes, nature-infused warmth. The company connects the world — this design connects to the earth. Think terra cotta, sage, warm cream, and living textures.

## Typography

- **Display**: Cormorant Garamond (Google Fonts, weights: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, with italic)
- **Body**: Lora (Google Fonts, weights: 400, 500, with italic)
- Warm, humanist, organic curves in every letterform

## Layout

- Flowing organic sections — no hard rectangles
- Circular and oval image crops
- Overlapping soft gradient blobs as section backgrounds
- Diagonal/wavy section transitions (CSS clip-path)
- Generous breathing room
- Nature-inspired decorative elements (leaf veining patterns in CSS)

## Animations

- Morphing SVG blob shapes
- Floating organic particles (pollen/dust motes)
- Gentle sway/float on hover (like leaves in breeze)
- Scroll-driven color temperature shifts (warm → cool → warm)
- Organic, breathing easing curves — never linear
- Elements "bloom" open on scroll (scale + fade)

## Tone

Warm, human, grounded. Anti-corporate. Natural materials feel. Calm confidence.
