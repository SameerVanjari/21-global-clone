# Art Deco Opulence

**Design Name**: Art Deco Opulence

**Concept**: 1920s Gatsby-era luxury reinterpreted for a global trading firm. Gold, geometry, and symmetry. The Roaring Twenties meet Dubai excess. Opulent but precise.

## Typography

- **Display**: Playfair Display (Google Fonts, weights: 400, 500, 600, 700, 900 — with italic variants)
- **Body**: Poppins (Google Fonts, weights: 200, 300, 400)
- Heavy use of italics for emphasis on Playfair

## Color Palette

- **Background**: `#0a0c10` (deepest navy-black)
- **Surface**: `#131620`
- **Gold Primary**: `#d4a853` (burnished gold)
- **Gold Light**: `#e8c97a`
- **Gold Dark**: `#b8942e`
- **Champagne**: `#efe0c0`
- **Cream**: `#faf6ed`
- **Navy**: `#1a2340`

## Layout

- Symmetrical, centered compositions
- Geometric sunburst/mandala decorative elements (CSS-only)
- Fan-shaped or stepped card layouts
- Thick gold-haired borders and rules
- Ornate horizontal dividers with diamond/lozenge shapes
- Step-pyramid (ziggurat) staggered sections
- Generous but formal whitespace

## Animations

- Gold shimmer/foil effect on hover (CSS gradient animation)
- Geometric pattern rotation on scroll
- Fan-shaped reveals (elements radiate outward)
- Stepped staggered animations with champagne sparkle
- Slow, luxurious easing curves
- Sparkle particle effects on key elements

## Tone

Maximalist luxury. Unapologetically opulent. Gold everywhere. Think Great Gatsby meets Burj Al Arab.
