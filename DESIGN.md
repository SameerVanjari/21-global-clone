# Architectural Monolith

**Design for Twenty1Global Trading LLC**

## Concept
The site as a building — concrete, shadow, mass. Monumental and unshakeable. Elements feel carved from stone. No decoration — only material, light, and void. Think Tadao Ando meets Dieter Rams.

## Typography
- Primary: Manrope (Google Fonts, weights: 200, 300, 400, 500, 600) — geometric, architectural
- Colossal headline sizes: `clamp(4rem, 12vw, 14rem)`
- Extreme thin weights (200) for headings
- Very wide letter-spacing on titles
- Body text at 300 weight

## Color Palette
- Background: `#f5f2ed` (warm concrete/limestone — not pure white)
- Surface Dark: `#1c1c1e` (near-black for contrast sections)
- Foreground: `#1a1a1a`
- Shadow Color: `#0a0a0a` (for deep ambient shadows)
- Muted: `#78776e` (warm gray)
- Border: none — mass and void only
- Accent: none — pure monochrome. The only "accent" is light and shadow.

## Layout
- Massive single-column flow
- Full-viewport sections — each section IS the viewport
- Elements that feel carved from solid material
- Dramatic negative space — sometimes a single word on screen
- Deep, ambient box-shadows (like architectural renderings)
- No borders — separation comes from material contrast (light section → dark section)
- Photography sized to architectural proportions (16:9, 2:1, full-bleed)

## Animations
- Slow, heavy scroll reveals — elements feel MASSIVE
- Ambient parallax with depth layering
- Shadow-casting on scroll (elements cast deeper shadows as they reveal)
- Content "emerges" from darkness — progressive disclosure from black
- Very long, slow easing curves (2-3 second transitions)
- Scale reveals (elements start slightly small and grow to full size)

## Tone
Monumental. Silent. Unshakeable. A fortress of a website. Timeless, like a stone building.
